# Emergency Preparedness & Family Continuity Protocol

## Overview

Most families suffer from financial fragmentation:
- Bank accounts, property deeds, life insurance policies, mortgage account numbers, and password vaults are scattered across multiple platforms.
- If an unforeseen emergency or incapacitation occurs, loved ones face weeks of legal friction and administrative confusion trying to locate vital accounts.

[Worth Diary](https://www.worthdiary.com) solves this with the **Family Emergency Continuity Protocol**.

---

## 1. The 3 Vital Pillars

Rather than overwhelming loved ones with hundreds of petty receipts, Worth Diary organizes finances into three actionable pillars:

1. **Money Flows**:
   - Captures fixed, recurring income streams (salary, pensions, dividends) and obligations (mortgages, term life insurance, rent, vehicle EMIs, subscriptions).
   - Normalizes frequencies into a clear monthly household baseline burn rate so surviving family members know exactly how much cash is needed to keep the home running.
2. **Possessions Directory**:
   - Centralized registry of assets (checking/savings, index funds, retirement accounts, real estate, vehicles, gold) and liabilities.
   - Maintained in native currencies with multi-currency conversion support.
   - Updated in 5 minutes every few months without bank scraping.
3. **Wishes & Directives**:
   - Confidential directives: master password vault locations, physical safe combinations, advance healthcare directives, insurance policy claim instructions, and attorney contact information.

---

## 2. Automated Encrypted PDF Dossier

- **AES-256 Encryption**: Generates an encrypted emergency PDF dossier containing the full household balance sheet, burn rate summary, and emergency directives.
- **Drive Folder Sharing**: Synchronizes directly to designated shared Google Drive folders for trusted emergency contacts (e.g., spouse, adult child, or estate attorney).
- **Zero App Required for Recipients**: Contacts do not need to install Worth Diary or create an account; they simply open the encrypted PDF in standard tools (Adobe Acrobat, Preview, Chrome).

---

## 3. Recurring Calendar Review Milestones

Emergency plans fail if family members forget where documents are saved. Worth Diary schedules gentle recurring Google Calendar review milestones (every 3, 6, or 12 months) directly into contacts' calendars:
- Contains direct links to their private Google Drive folder.
- Includes a 2-minute quick checklist to ensure contact numbers and account locations remain fresh without awkward family conversations.
